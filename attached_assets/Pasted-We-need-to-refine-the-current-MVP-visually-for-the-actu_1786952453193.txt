We need to refine the current MVP visually for the actual restaurant: **Rodina**.

Do NOT rebuild the UX or change the existing feedback flow. Keep the current mobile-first interaction and structure intact. This is a visual/brand refinement pass.

Rodina's official brand colours are:

* Primary Dark Forest Green: #1B4D3E
* Alternative darker green: #143B2E
* Accent Neon Lime: #CCFF00
* Alternative lime: #D4FF1A
* White: #FFFFFF

Apply the following visual hierarchy:

### 1. Background — approximately 60%

Use pure white (#FFFFFF) or a very subtle warm/off-white background.

The customer feedback experience should feel clean, calm, premium and effortless.

### 2. Structural elements — approximately 30%

Use Rodina's Dark Forest Green (#1B4D3E or #143B2E) for:

* primary text
* headings where appropriate
* progress indicators
* subtle borders
* icons
* structural UI elements
* any small brand/navigation elements

Remove the current coral/orange visual treatment and replace it with Rodina's green system.

### 3. CTA / interaction emphasis — approximately 10%

Use the Neon Lime (#CCFF00 or #D4FF1A) sparingly.

Reserve it primarily for important interactive actions such as:

* Continue
* Submit Feedback
* Give Rating
* Finish

Use dark forest green or near-black text on the lime buttons for readability.

Do NOT use neon lime everywhere. It should function as a high-visibility action colour.

### Brand personality

Rodina should feel:

* premium
* warm
* modern
* confident
* understated
* hospitality-focused

Avoid making it feel like a generic SaaS dashboard.

Avoid excessive gradients, glassmorphism, excessive shadows, unnecessary animations, or overly technical UI.

The customer should feel like they are simply giving Rodina a quick "gut check", not filling out a corporate survey.

### Important UX constraint

Do NOT increase complexity.

Keep the central product principle:

**Ridiculously easy for the customer.**

The visual design should support that principle rather than compete with it.

Make the result feel like a polished hospitality experience rather than a software product.

After applying the visual refinement, preserve the existing functionality and ensure the entire flow remains responsive on mobile and desktop.
