---
name: Rodina visual identity
description: Brand direction for the restaurant customer feedback experience.
---

Rodina's feedback experience should feel premium, warm, modern, confident, understated, and hospitality-focused. Use white or a very subtle warm white for the majority of the surface, dark forest green for structure and text, and neon lime only for high-visibility actions.

**Why:** The product is a QR-opened customer experience, not a SaaS dashboard or corporate survey. Visual restraint protects the core promise that feedback should be quick and effortless.

**How to apply:** Keep the interaction model simple and unchanged when making future visual refinements. Avoid coral/orange treatment, excessive gradients, glass effects, heavy shadows, and unnecessary animation. Brand colors: #1B4D3E, #143B2E, #CCFF00, #D4FF1A, #FFFFFF.
